## Aerofly Startgerät / Missionsgerät (reference only)

FlightMate’s live weather, SimBrief import, and `main.mcf` apply features
were implemented by studying and porting algorithms from:

- [fboes/aerofly-startgeraet](https://github.com/fboes/aerofly-startgeraet)
